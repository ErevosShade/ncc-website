ABOUT THE UNIT

A legacy built on
purpose and service.

NCC at BIT Mesra is part of a wider national movement that encourages young people to become disciplined, confident and service-minded citizens.

The unit's own history reaches back to January 1956, when it was raised at Sindri as an EME section. It moved to Mesra in January 1957, then evolved through several technical formations before taking its present Jharkhand designation following the formation of the state.

OUR JOURNEY

A timeline of
transformation.

From a small EME section to a composite technical company, each chapter reflects an organisation adapting while carrying forward its purpose.

1956
01 · THE BEGINNING

Raised at Sindri

On 5 January 1956, the unit was raised at Sindri, Bihar, as 6 Bihar EME Section NCC to provide NCC coverage to the Bihar Institute of Technology, Sindri.

1957
02 · A NEW HOME

Relocation to Mesra

On 1 January 1957, the unit moved to its present location at Mesra, Ranchi, to provide NCC coverage to the Birla Institute of Technology.

1964
03 · REORGANISATION

1 Bihar EME Unit

In July 1964, the unit was reorganised and its reported cadet strength increased to 120. It received the designation 1 Bihar EME Unit, Mesra (Ranchi).

1967
04 · EXPANSION

The Mixed Regiment era

In November 1967, Engineers, Signals and EME technical companies were amalgamated. The formation was upgraded to a Mixed Regiment with a reported strength of 600 cadets and renamed 3 Bihar Mixed Regiment NCC.

ENGINEERSSIGNALSEME
1977
05 · A NEW STRUCTURE

Composite Technical Company

Under a reduction policy for NCC technical units, the regiment was reduced to a company. Technical companies were reorganised into platoons and the reported strength reduced from 600 to 200. The unit became 3 Bihar Composite Technical Company NCC.

2000+
06 · JHARKHAND

The present designation

Following the formation of the state of Jharkhand, the unit was redesignated 3 Jharkhand Composite Technical Company NCC, Mesra.

Frequently asked

Use these answers as a starting point, then confirm current policy and dates through official NCC channels.

What is the full name of the unit? +

3 Jharkhand Composite Technical Company, NCC, BIT Mesra, Ranchi.

When was the unit established? +

The unit's supplied history states that it was raised on 5 January 1956 at Sindri as 6 Bihar EME Section NCC. It moved to Mesra on 1 January 1957.

How can I join NCC at BIT Mesra? +

Enrolment schedules, eligibility and vacancies can change. Check current official BIT Mesra notices and contact the unit through verified institute channels.

What training does NCC include? +

NCC can include drill, physical training, field craft, technical instruction, camps and community service. Specific training depends on the authorised syllabus and unit schedule.

Are the activities shown on this website current? +

The activities shown here are historical examples supported by institute or Directorate records, mainly from 2024. They are not a live or upcoming events calendar.
