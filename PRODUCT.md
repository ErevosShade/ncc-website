# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
- **Primary:** Current cadets (Senior Division & Senior Wing), students of Birla Institute of Technology (BIT) Mesra, and prospective student recruits exploring NCC life.
- **Secondary:** Unit alumni, faculty, Associate NCC Officers (ANOs), and inspecting officers from Ranchi Group Headquarters and NCC Directorate Bihar & Jharkhand.

## Product Purpose
The official institutional display website for **3 Jharkhand Composite Technical Company NCC**, located at Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra, Ranchi. The site serves as the digital front door showcasing unit history, training philosophy, outreach activities, ceremonial parades, notices, and photographic records.

## Positioning
The exclusive official web presence for the composite technical NCC unit at BIT Mesra (Engineers, Signals, and EME platoons raised in 1956). Unlike national recruitment portals, this site is purely an authentic institutional showcase—no online enrolments or internal application workflows take place here.

## Operating Context
- Browsed on desktop and mobile by students, faculty, alumni, and visiting officers.
- Visited to review unit legacy, past ceremonial events (Independence Day, Kargil Vijay Diwas, Puneet Sagar Abhiyan), camp achievements, and visual gallery.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- **Display Only:** Purely informational and showcase; no user accounts, login portals, or form enrolments on this site.
- **5 Core Institutional Views:**
  1. Home / Unit Overview
  2. Legacy & History (1956 Sindri to BIT Mesra journey)
  3. Official Notices & Circulars (Display only)
  4. Training & Outreach Activities
  5. Photographic Gallery (Parades, camps, social drives)
- **Audio Experience:** Headless, keyboard-triggered NCC Anthem (`play` / `amrit` to start, `stop` to halt). Zero visual distraction.

## Brand Commitments
- **Design Language:** Official Government of India & NCC institutional portal language modeled after `indiancc.mygov.in`.
- **Identity Assets:** Official transparent NCC Crest (`/ncc-logo.svg`), tricolour bands (Army Red `#ef1c24`, Navy Blue `#2e3092`, Air Force Sky Blue `#00aeef`), gold accents (`#ffc80b`).
- **Motto:** "Unity and Discipline / एकता और अनुशासन".
- **Hero Centerpiece:** Real photograph of unit cadets in front of the red institutional signboard at BIT Mesra (`/home.jpg`), unobstructed by heavy overlays or clutter.

## Evidence on Hand
- Unit photographic archive: `public/home.jpg`, `public/gallery/*.jpg`.
- Official SVG Crest: `public/ncc-logo.svg`.
- Historical dates & platoon citations (Corps of Engineers, Signals, EME).
- Documented outreach events (78th Independence Day, Puneet Sagar Abhiyan, Kargil Vijay Diwas, Swachh Bharat).

## Product Principles
1. **Authenticity Over Generic Slop:** Showcase real cadets, genuine institutional history, and accurate unit designations rather than generic stock imagery.
2. **Institutional Dignity:** Adhere to clean, formal government design standards with high contrast, legible typography, and no gimmicky widgets.
3. **Information Clarity:** Maintain clear, static presentation of notices, achievements, and platoon structures without transactional complexity.
4. **Unobtrusive Media:** Present media with zero intrusive overlays or visual noise.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
- WCAG AA compliant contrast across light and dark themes.
- Accessibility topbar with font size controls (A- / A / A+), high-contrast toggle, and screen-reader skip links.
- Semantic HTML landmarks with proper ARIA labeling across all 5 views.
